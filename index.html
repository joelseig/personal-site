About.

the short version

I'm Commercial & Legal Director at VIDA Roads, currently working on the North East Link Central Package — Victoria's largest PPP/ITC road project. Before that, six years on the West Gate Tunnel through delivery and closeout. Before VIDA, the Property, Infrastructure and Projects team at the University of Melbourne, after a stint in private practice.

The thread running through what I write here is knowledge work in major-project delivery, and what the AI tooling cycle has to do with it. Risk, claims and disputes; the documents and reference layers that hold a project together; and why portable, plain-text artefacts tend to age better than vendor workflows.

If you want to get in touch about major-project work, writing, or AI tooling, email is best.
