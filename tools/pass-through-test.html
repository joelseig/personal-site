How this is calculated: each profile's cost = fresh input × input rate + cached re-reads × the cache-read percentage you set + output × output rate; the blended seat cost weights the three profiles by your usage mix. Published cache-read discounts range from about 2.5% to 10% of the input rate by provider and tier: Anthropic's flagship tier reads cache at 2.5% and its mid tiers at 5% after the September–October cuts, OpenAI's Sol at 5%, Google at 10%. The 10% default is the dear end of the published range; lower it to match your model. The metered lab route adds the seat fee to whichever model you've marked as the route. Open-weight models: if you self-host, convert the hosting bill into effective per-million rates and enter them on the open-weight row (a modest single-node GPU endpoint runs in the order of US$4,700 a month; a frontier-class open-weight model needs a multi-GPU node at US$11,000 a month and up; divide by your monthly tokens); the engineering field covers the people. The team total multiplies the per-seat route by your seat count, applies your discount to the platform seats only, and adds your engineering figure to the two lab routes (a raw model doesn't do legal work out of the box; the default US$500,000 is an editable assumption, not a quote). What it leaves out: the legal platforms bundle several frontier models, legal workflow, security and support behind one contract; the flat lab seat carries fair-use limits; lab enterprise deals carry seat minimums (a reported 150 for ChatGPT Enterprise). Platform seat prices are reported, not published. Spot rates are published list as at 10 October 2026 and change often, which is why you can edit them. Currency: model rates are entered in US$ because that is how every lab publishes them. In A$ mode the seat prices and engineering figure are read as A$, everything is converted at the exchange rate you set, and switching currency converts the figures already entered. These products are billed in US$, so an Australian buyer carries the exchange rate on top of everything else. This is arithmetic, not advice.
